## 1. What went wrong

The biggest losses were calendar time, compute and owner attention, not modelling choices.

**1. Compute was limited by our own choices, and even the free compute was under-used.** Cost: ~0.010–0.020 LB never built (estimate).
- Roughly 90 of the ~270 free T4-hours since early August were used.
- Kaggle bills the session, not the GPU. The 10-03 arithmetic confirms it: 30 − 12.83 = 17.2 h charged for a 7.6 h 1-GPU run plus a 9.1 h 2-GPU run.
- The CoAtNet student, c6 and dread ran on one T4 of two (build_backbone_ab_kernel.py:326-327 sets NPROC=1 unless stage_b).
  - dread was single-GPU by design, to isolate the label change (AUDIT.md:255-258).
  - The CoAtNet student a week later had no such reason.
  - The ResNet teachers ran on the Mac, so they wasted no Kaggle GPU (the draft was wrong to count them).
- dread and c6 stopped at 8 epochs because of the 12 h session cap.
- The Mac sat idle from 09-29 to 10-02 (RUNBOOK.md "Running now").
- Cloud GPUs were priced at $150–250 for ~100 A100-h on 09-24 and declined on 09-27 (archive RUNBOOK-2026-09-28:1218, :132).

**2. Dormancy, and a 2–6 week lag between diagnosis and action.** Cost: every lever now in the score arrived late. When active, the project went from 0.914 to 0.939 in about 11 days.
- 32 of 58 days have no recorded work.
- No submission was made between 08-18 and 09-26.
- Lags from diagnosis to action:

  | Lever | Diagnosed → acted on | Lag |
  |---|---|---|
  | Dense slices | 08-18 → 09-24 | 37 days |
  | Labels as the lever | 08-13 → 09-27 | 45 days |
  | Copying the frontier's training script | by 09-06 → 09-23 | 17+ days |
  | All-data training | 09-23 → 10-03 | 10 days |

**3. The label lever was dismissed, and its cheapest form never ran.**
- D4 ("labels are not the binding constraint") was restored on 09-06 (DATA.md:39-60).
- Stage L, a frontier-LLM criteria read priced at $25–150 (DATA.md:257-275), was scheduled for 09-07..09-15 and never run.
- The LB reversed D4 on 09-27 (dread_soft: 0.901 → 0.916).
- We still do not know what produced dread_soft (AUDIT.md:141). That is the first label question to answer, before spending anything.

**4. The leaderboard, the only large image-graded instrument, went largely unused.**
- About 13 submissions out of ~290 allowed.
- DECISIONS.md:87 still says "a submission costs ~2 h", although RUNBOOK.md:244 retracted it.
- The correct cost is not zero. Each new build needs a commit run of ~0.1–0.25 quota-h (the --ours-only GATE rebuilds 8 studies from DICOMs, build_submit_kernel.py:201-212), plus 30–45 attended minutes.
- The hidden rerun itself is free.

**5. Gates were aimed at questions they could not answer.** Cost: ~17 GPU-h and the week of 10-02, with no LB proof.
- Round 1's gate scored report + teacher targets on gold-58: 0.887 → 0.913, P 0.994.
- That ensemble cannot exist at test time.
- It scored a steven_v2 + teacher artifact, while the students trained on dread_soft + teacher (archive RUNBOOK:543-545; fusion/local_teacher_folds.py:24).
- The students read flat: CoAtNet student 0.931 vs the core's 0.933; Ortho student 0.927 vs ~0.930 if input and labels simply added (RUNBOOK.md forecast block).
- Gold said +0.026, the LB said ~0 to +0.005. Even in direction-only agreement, the magnitudes were off 2–20× (c7→dread gold +0.022 vs LB +0.001; dread→r1 gold −0.001 vs LB +0.011).

**6. We modified things before copying them exactly.**
- D3 blamed a 0.085 gap on NIfTI files and the 458 GB corpus was deleted. The real cause was input sampling (DECISIONS.md:17, :124).
- train_windows still differs from the frontier in three deliberate ways (train_windows.py:25-38):
  - no slot-straddling windows;
  - a confidence weight `0.25+0.75|2y−1|` on every target type (:755-760);
  - no gold epoch pick.
- (The draft wrongly listed k=12 random windows as a fourth difference. The frontier also trains on 12 random windows, MODEL.md:24.)
- The confidence weight multiplies with pos_weight (up to 10). It cuts dread_soft's 0.45 level to 0.33 and teacher-vs-report conflict cells to 0.25.
- Stage A dread Ortho (0.916) vs the frontier core (0.928) has never been broken down with an exact-copy control.

**7. The endgame was built inside someone else's runtime, and that runtime was never measured.**
- The stack takes 8–9 h of the 9 h limit, and its per-cell cost is untimed (RUNBOOK.md Open item 4).
- Yet submit-to-score elapsed times already bound branch runtimes for free:
  - the core alone (2 passes): ≤79 min;
  - Stage B Ortho alone: ≤39 min (RUNBOOK.md:178-180, submitted 06:50/07:06, read 08:09/07:45).
- The CoAtNet student was a 7-epoch, lr 3e-5 restart from a converged SWA checkpoint (2.4% relative weight change). It was always going to land near the core.

**8. Closed solo resources, heavy process overhead, and ops stalls.**
- No merger, no radiologist, no host question in 7 weeks, no API spend.
- 13,390 lines of markdown, and 39 of the 50 commits since 09-28 are docs-only.
- About 13 h of critical-path stalls in 10 days: a 7 h permission prompt on 09-25, a ~12 h Mac sleep on 10-02/03, plus a GPU-only log line that killed a run (RUNBOOK.md:136-147).

**The recurring pattern, still active.** The process guards hard against false positives, which are cheap at our rank. It lets in false negatives, scarcity and scope sprawl, and those are what cost us. This plan's first draft repeated the third: about 40 items against a solo owner's demonstrated throughput.

| Pattern | Instances |
|---|---|
| A careful measurement aimed at a question it cannot answer | The round-2 gold gate; a 224-px ResNet (~0.883 LB-equivalent) proposed to judge labels for 0.93 models |
| An underpowered null closed by a rule | VLM (vision tower got no gradient); small CNN (forum recipe never run); NYU-N (weak teacher); zoom (one seed) |
| Scarcity treated as a law | One big run a week; ~1 submission a day; the 8–9 h stack treated as fixed |
| Scope beyond capacity (new in this review) | ~20 new code paths planned at "~2 h a day" |

## 5. The honest odds

**Assumed lines on 10-22 (public scale).**

|---|---|---|
| Gold | top ~20 | ~0.961–0.964 |
| Silver | top ~250 | ~0.948–0.950 |

**Bottom-up arithmetic.**
- **Final 2 = CoAt branch + Ortho family.**
  - Inputs: CoAt branch alone (unread) ~0.937–0.941; Ortho family ~0.937–0.942.
  - Two ~0.94 families at ρ ~0.85 gain ~+0.004–0.006 over their mean, less a ~0.001 selection discount.
  - Result: **0.944–0.948**.
- **Final 1 = the stack + Ortho family at 1/3.**
  - Rate fitted from Sub B-dread at ρ ≈ 0.89: a 1/3 member alone at ~0.940 adds +0.001–0.003, giving **0.944–0.946**.
  - With a ~0.945 family (a T2/T3 win plus 3 members) it adds ~+0.004, giving **~0.947**.
- **Neither reaches the gold line.** It needs a single model ~0.950+ (P ≈ 15%) or a strong partner.

| Path | Plausible public | P(silver+) | P(gold) | P(win) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Status quo (this morning's plan) | 0.943–0.946 | ~20% | <1% | <0.2% |

**Reading this.**
- An outright win is not a realistic target on any path.

- Our forecasts have run high (gold +0.026 became ~0 on the LB; (A) was forecast at 0.943–0.946), so read every range as optimistic by ~0.002.
