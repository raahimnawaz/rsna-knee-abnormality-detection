## 2. The verified mistakes

**How these were checked (10-03).** Each was put to one adversarial checker that read the code and docs, cited
file:line, tried to refute the claim, and priced the fix. None was refuted outright; several are smaller than the
brainstorm claimed. Full record, including the refutation attempts:
[`docs/archive/brainstorm-2026-10-03/mistakes-verified.json`](archive/brainstorm-2026-10-03/mistakes-verified.json).
Gains are on the public LB and are estimates unless marked measured.

### 2.1 Ensembling was never done, and was priced from a comment (confirmed)

Every model we have trained is a single seed. Fold and seed ensembling was priced at "+0.001" from a remark by the core's
author (MODEL.md:33-35). Our own data said otherwise:
- the 4-fold ResNet34 mean read 0.832 on the 691, against 0.814–0.819 for single folds (archive RUNBOOK:546);
- the stack's denser, slice-reversed inference added **+0.005 LB** to the same CoAtNet weights (0.928 → 0.933, measured).

The Ortho seed noise has never been measured.

- **Fix:** every final family is 2–3 all-data seeds, served through one multi-weight notebook. Add a reversed/denser pass
  to Ortho inference as test-time augmentation. First check that reversing the slices does not swap medial and lateral
  under Stage B's canonical orientation.
- **Worth:** +0.003–0.006 per family. **The most reliable item here.**
- **Cost:** ~9–10 T4-h per extra Ortho seed. The reversed pass needs ~1–2 engineering hours and a submission.
- **Fastest test:**
  1. The reversed pass on existing Ortho weights: one submission, no training.
  2. Then Ortho-all seed 2: submit seed 1 alone against rank-mean(seed 1, seed 2). A gain of ≥ +0.004 is decisive;
     under +0.002 caps the lever.

### 2.2 The teacher round was checked against the wrong baseline (confirmed; the LB has now shown it twice)

- **The gate's mistake.** It compared report+teacher targets (gold 0.913) with the report alone (0.887). The right
  baseline was the model already trained on the reports: `dread` reads 0.912 on gold, so the real gain was **+0.001**.
- **Why same-label teachers cannot help.** A student learns E[target | image]. With a teacher trained on the same
  reports, that is ≈ E[report | image], which the student learns anyway.
- **Why the CoAtNet student test was weak.** It was a 7-epoch, lr 3e-5 restart from the converged SWA core, so three
  things changed at once.
- **LB:** (B) −0.002, and (A) 0.943 equals the stack. Ortho r1 read 0.927 against ~0.930 if input and labels had simply
  added.
- **Still open:** T1 trains on these round-1 targets, with the confidence weight (§2.6).
- **Evidence:**
  - `labeling/build_student_targets.py:12-16,137-138,185-190`;
  - `fusion/local_teacher_folds.py:23-24` (the teacher trains on `dread_soft`);
  - archive RUNBOOK:527-528 and :542-546;
  - DATA.md:600-603;
  - `fusion/runs_dread_ortho/summary.json` (gold 0.9121).
- **Fix, as standing rules:**
  1. Check any label or target change against the incumbent model's own gold read, never against the report alone.
  2. Targets that contain an image model's output cannot be gated on gold at all.
  3. Same-label teacher rounds stay closed.
  4. T1 vs T2 (§3) decides whether the round-1 targets stay in the finals.
- **Worth:** ~0 directly. It saves the 20–37 quota-h a round 2 would have cost.

### 2.4 Training on 12 windows out of 54–82 was never tested (partly)

**What happens now.** Each training step draws k = 12 random windows per study, unstratified
(`fusion/train_windows.py:280-282`, default `--k 12` at :526). Scoring uses every window.

**Coverage.** Stage A has 54 windows; Stage B has 82, ~76 in a typical study (`pipeline/slots96.py:30-41`). Stage B
lowered training coverage from 22% to 16%, and nobody checked it.

**What that costs a lesion (hypergeometric, at N = 76):**

| A lesion visible on… | k = 12: missing from this share of positive bags | k = 24 |
|---|--:|--:|
| 6 windows | 34% | 9% |
| 2–4 windows | 50–71% | |

**Possible edge.** The frontier also trains at k = 12, so fixing this could put us ahead of everyone.

**Caveat.** The −0.048 figure (12 evenly spaced windows vs 54) is an inference measurement on the same weights, not a
training result.

**Fix:**
1. **No training-run quota.** Dump r1's per-window features (all windows, 3,502 train + 691; ~1.7 h on T4×2). On the
   Mac, refit the head with k = 12 bags against full bags, 2 seeds each, paired on the 691. If full bags win, swap that
   head into the final Ortho: same keys, same inference code.
2. **Then:** `--k 24 --micro 1`. Per-forward memory is the same as now, so it needs no code. An all-data Ortho at k = 24
   is ~16.5 h, which needs resume support or a cloud GPU.

**Worth:** +0.000–0.005 on a final (a single model up to +0.006), mostly Lateral Meniscus, MCL, Lateral OA and PF OA.
Speculative.

### 2.5 The small CNN was tested in the wrong setup (partly)

**What happened.** ResNet34 ran inside the ViT setup, unlike the recipe two top-100 competitors report at 0.94+
(ResNet34 / EffNet-B0, 224–288 px, simple pooling):

| Difference | Where |
|---|---|
| A 12-way attention-MIL head | `train_windows.py:339-410` |
| k = 12 windows | |
| BatchNorm statistics from 24 windows of 2 knees (micro 2) | :526-528, :814 |
| 336 → 224 bilinear resize without antialiasing | :374 |

- The 09-27 FAIL was explicitly scoped as "not a verdict on small CNNs" (archive :303-317). RUNBOOK:282 dropped that
  scope.
- The same CNN then became the round-1 teacher and the screening model for Z, R and N.

**Fix:**
1. **Serve the 4 recipe-C ResNet folds alone through `--ours-only`.** `infer_windows.build_model` builds any timm model
   from `last_meta`; smoke it first. This calibrates the CNN's 691 → LB mapping with no training.
2. **One Mac night: a forum-recipe arm.**
   - Settings: `--head meanmax` (new), antialias on, `--micro 8 --bs 8` or frozen BN, k = 24, 288 px, ~15 epochs,
     `dread_soft`.
   - Plus a second B′ seed to measure seed noise.
   - Rule: forum arm − mean(B′ seeds) ≥ +0.010 on the 691 → build a 5-fold family. It joins a final only at LB ≥ 0.930.

**Worth:** +0.001–0.005 directly. More as an enabler: a cheap model for fast iteration and a third family.
P(a 5-fold family reads ≥ 0.925–0.930 LB) ≈ 35–50%.

**Cost:** 3–4 engineering hours; 7–10 Mac-h.

### 2.6 The loss weighting was carried over from older labels (partly)

**What it does.** The loss is multiplied by `0.25 + 0.75|2y − 1|` with no flag to turn it off
(`train_windows.py:752-761`).
- Its only stated reason is `steven_v2`, where 0.5 means "the report does not say" (:31-32).
- It is also applied to `dread_soft` (0.45 → weight 0.325; 0.20 → 0.70) and to teacher tables, where conflict cells get
  0.25–0.36.
- The frontier has no such term (MODEL.md:28).
- We built it ourselves from pilkwang's separate confidence column (`train_port.py:173-176`).

**Fix:** add `--conf-weight {fork,none}`. Use `none` for `dread_soft` and every later table, riding along in T2. Do not
spend an LB slot on it alone.

**Worth:** +0.000–0.004, sign unknown.

### 2.7 We never copied the frontier recipe exactly (partly)

- **The unexplained gap.** The 0.012 gap between `dread` (0.916) and the public core (0.928) was explained by assertion
  (archive :36). One of the reasons given, "gold ×3", does not exist.
- **It may be larger.** "Ortho > CoAtNet by +0.010 at equal setup" compared Ortho with our own harness's ImageNet
  CoAtNet. So the real setup deficit may be ~0.02.
- **Averaged weights are thrown away.** The last-3-epoch weights are scored and then discarded
  (`train_windows.py:917-930`).

**Fix:**
1. **Free Mac test.** B′ against a frontier-setup arm (confidence weight off, plus `--straddle` windows that cross series
   boundaries), paired on the 691. Rule: F − B′ ≥ +0.008 at P ≥ 0.95 → test each flag separately.
2. **Ship averaged weights.** Finals use the last-3 (or last-k) averaged weights.

**Worth:** +0.000–0.004. There is a ~15% chance it exposes a ≥ 0.010 setup defect that affects every model we train.

### 2.8 We measured results wrongly (partly)

**(a) Gold-58 overstated gains.** Used as a gate, it priced round 1 at +0.026; the LB gave ~0 to +0.005.

**(b) The 691 tracks the LB for held-out Ortho runs.** Fit: **LB = 1.091 × 691 − 0.004**.
- Fit quality: r 0.996, residuals ≤ 0.0014, leave-one-out error ≤ 0.0019, over 4 points that include label and teacher
  changes.
- Gold-58 on the same 4 points: r 0.845, leave-one-out error up to 0.013.
- On this fit, 0.950 needs **0.874** on the 691 and 0.965 needs **0.888**.
- So rule 3's blanket "the 691 cannot judge labels" is contradicted by our own data. Four points justify a pre-registered
  screen, not a judge.

**(c) The LB was rationed by a stale note.** `DECISIONS.md:87` still says "a submission costs ~2 h". Only the commit run
uses quota (~0.1–0.25 h); the hidden rerun is free. We used ~13 of ~290 slots.

**(d) One-finding fixes can be measured.** `DATA.md:682` says no instrument can confirm one. That is wrong: a
single-column swap has a macro error of ~0.0006–0.0026, so a +0.03 fix on one finding is 2–3σ on a common or mid
finding. That is the instrument for the weak four.

**Proposed policy:**
- The 691 is the pre-registered screen for held-out single Ortho models. Pass at paired Δ ≥ +0.007.
- Gold-58 never gates. It is reported only, on trained models.
- The LB gets 3–5 pre-registered reads a day.
- Never pick among more than 3 near-equal variants, and never pick per finding by public score.

### 2.9 Runs used one of the two T4s (partly)

- **The cause:** `notebooks/build_backbone_ab_kernel.py:326-327` starts one process unless the layout is Stage B.
- **The cost:** ~21 session-hours ran on one T4 after multi-GPU training existed (dread 11.1, CoAtNet student 7.6,
  ResNets 2.4). The direct LB cost is ~0.
- **Fix:**
  - use every GPU on every CUDA run;
  - assert `world == device_count` in full mode, so a single-GPU run fails loudly;
  - add an option to turn checkpointing off for CoAtNet.

### 2.10 The VLM was shelved for a bug, not a result (partly)

**What happened.** The Qwen3.5-2B VLM read 0.5041 on the 691 with constant predictions. Its vision blocks moved 4.4e-4,
about what weight decay alone does, so they got no gradient (archive :929-946). The cause was never found:
- the "fp16 underflow" refutation compared per-weight gradients, which is the wrong quantity;
- the 15-minute diagnostic was never run.

**Unverified:** the 54th-place "0.950 single" claim is a forum self-report.

**Fix:**
1. A per-group gradient-norm run (vision blocks / merger / LoRA / head), with and without fp16 autocast on the vision
   tower.
2. Then an fp32 vision tower, or bf16 on a cloud GPU.
3. Then the 1-epoch check (bar: 0.75 on the 691). Expect 2.4 h per epoch on T4×2.

**Worth:** if it works, a third family at +0.005–0.008. P(working by 10-15) ≈ 20–25%.
