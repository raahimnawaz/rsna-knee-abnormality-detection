# Review 2026-10-12: where the error is, and what would remove it (goal 0.97)

**Read after `RUNBOOK.md`.** The RUNBOOK wins on state. This file is the diagnosis and the proposed work, written
after today's reads (five folds 0.945; labels +0.005; Synovitis 0.884 on the LB; outside teacher 0.920 on gold).
**Nothing in §5 is approved or run.** Every number on gold-58 is report-only (rule 1) and is used here to explain,
never to select.

## 0. The short version

1. **Four findings carry ~70% of what is left**: Lateral OA, Synovitis, PF OA, Lateral Meniscus (gold-58, five folds).
   The other eight read 0.946–0.986. The one LB probe we have agrees in size (Synovitis 0.884 = 16% of fold 0's deficit).
2. **They fail for two different reasons.** On Lateral OA and PF OA (and MCL, ACL) the *report* reads gold better than
   our *image* model: the answer is in our labels and the model does not see it. On Synovitis, Effusion, Contusion,
   Fracture, Baker's the image model already beats the report: the labels are the limit. Lateral Meniscus is both.
3. **The vision limit has a mechanical cause in our cache**: we keep 87% of sagittal slices but 53% of coronal and
   44% of axial, so coronal is sampled every 6.5 mm and axial every 9.0 mm. Lateral OA, MCL (coronal) and PF OA
   (axial) are the findings read on those planes. Add: a global average over each 384 px slice, 12 of ~76 windows per
   training step, no geometric augmentation.
4. **It is not** the scanner, field strength or report language (held-out macro 0.83–0.89 across all of them), and not
   backbone size.
5. A public ConvNeXt-small (the same backbone as ours) ships its trainer. It differs from ours in exactly the places
   §2 points at: every slice, knee-anchored compartment views, spatial attention, 176 windows per study, mirror and
   affine augmentation.
6. **Outlook, stated plainly:** recovering the report-level signal on the vision-limited findings is worth about
   +0.008–0.012 macro (§6). That takes our own ensemble to ~0.953–0.957. 0.97 needs the weak four near 0.93, which no
   report-trained model has shown; it needs image-graded supervision (what OAI gives the public 0.949 model).

## 3. The clinical reading (why these four, plane by plane)

- **PF OA.** Patellar and trochlear cartilage are read on the axial fluid-sensitive series, with the sagittal for
  the trochlea. The patella is ~40 mm tall: at one axial slice per 9 mm it is on 4–5 of our slices, and a focal
  defect on one or none. MRI itself is weaker on the trochlea than the patella (72% against 87% sensitivity in a
  review of arthroscopy-controlled studies), so the labels are softer here too.
- **Lateral OA.** Lateral tibiofemoral cartilage loss is rarer than medial (reference prevalence 26% against 36%)
  and is read on the coronal series and the lateral sagittal slices. Coronal is sampled every 6.5 mm. A generic
  "tricompartmental OA" report gives all three compartments the same label (`9c23daf:DATA.md` §12.8b), which adds
  label noise to exactly this compartment.
- **Lateral Meniscus.** The hardest structure for MRI itself: pooled sensitivity ~78% against ~91% for the medial
  meniscus (75 studies, 8,517 knees), because of the popliteal hiatus, small radial and root tears, and the
  posterior horn in ACL-deficient knees. The report is therefore wrong more often, and the image model inherits it.
  Report and image err on different knees (0.910 together): the errors are partly independent.
- **Synovitis.** The reference standard is contrast-enhanced MRI; on a non-contrast study it is inferred from
  effusion and synovial thickening, and the Hoffa-fat-pad sign is sensitive but not specific. Reports address it in
  14–22% of studies. It is a label problem and partly an irreducible one.
- **MCL.** Visible only on the coronal fluid-sensitive series, at the edge of the field, over 10–15 mm front to
  back: about two of our slices. A third of reports describe MCL signal below the grading threshold.

## 4. Outside sources, and what each one supports

**4.1 The public ConvNeXt-small (`heliosli`, R7; `manifest.json` in `heliosli/rsna-knee-compact-r7cnxfsss`, trainer
embedded in the notebook `heliosli/0-957-latest-dataset-score-in-one-click`).** Its standalone LB is not published;
50/50 with the public CoAtNet it reads ≈0.957 (its earlier S6 at 30%: 0.954 against 0.949). Recipe against ours:

| | public R7 | ours |
|---|---|---|
| slices | every slice, 3.5 mm physical step | fixed 96, half of coronal / axial dropped |
| views | full field + 100 mm knee-anchored locals: coronal medial and lateral, patellofemoral, posterior | one 140 mm field |
| pooling | per-finding **spatial** attention inside a window, then over windows | global average, then over windows |
| windows per study, training | **176**, cover sampling (every series at stride 3, random phase), batch 2 | **12** random, batch 8 |
| augmentation | random crop 352 of 384, rotation / scale, gamma / contrast, slice jitter, **mirror with Medial ↔ Lateral outputs swapped** (MCL not trained on a mirrored batch) | gain jitter |
| loss | BCE + report-state head (0.5) + matched-pair ranking (0.5) + OA extent (0.2) + Synovitis = own part + β · stop-grad(Effusion) + fastMRI+ lesion boxes as an auxiliary task (0.3) | BCE |
| weights | EMA 0.999, 16 epochs, wd 0.05 | last epoch, 12 epochs, wd 0.02 |
| folds | 4 | 5 |

Its author's note on why the patellofemoral view exists: a local crop at the field centre cuts the patella at its
edge. That is the likely reason our centre zoom (arm Z, 09-29) and the public CoAtNet's crop-trained model (0.946
against 0.949) did not pay: the centre is not where PF OA is.

**4.2 The public CoAtNet (`nartaa`, 0.949 alone).** Same head and sampler as ours (its script is our trainer's
ancestor: global average, k = 12, gain jitter only). What it has that we do not: 2,399 OAI knees with masked
supervision, all 4,349 studies in one model, 16 epochs, the mirror pass at test (+0.001). So its edge over our single
model is data, not design.

**4.3 One team's public ledger (`prvsiyan`, 90 submissions).** Things that tied or lost on top of the 0.949 reader:
a 30% ConvNeXt 2.5D blend, a 224 px view, four-view test-time augmentation, a MaViT reader (0.935), frozen-encoder
overlays. What stuck earlier: depth-index alignment (+0.008), the mirror reader (+0.008 over their old stack).

**4.4 Literature.**
- Knee MRI against arthroscopy: medial meniscus sensitivity ~91%, lateral ~78.5%; ACL ~87% / 93%.
- Cartilage: sensitivity 70–80% pooled, specificity 95–99%; patellar 87% against trochlear 72%.
- Synovitis without contrast: effusion-synovitis is the better surrogate; Hoffa signal is non-specific.
- fastMRI+: 16,154 radiologist boxes over 22 knee pathology classes on 1,172 fastMRI knee volumes (coronal). Image
  labels, public with the NYU agreement. Ours as a *teacher* failed its gate; R7 uses it as an *auxiliary task*.
- RSNA 2024 lumbar spine: the top solutions were multi-stage (find the level, crop, then grade).
- Deep AUC maximisation (AUC-margin loss, LibAUC): pre-train with cross-entropy, fine-tune on an AUC surrogate;
  first on CheXpert in 2020. A direct fit to an AUROC metric; untested here.
- MRNet (1,370 exams): a slice-pooled CNN per series; its released labels are report-extracted.

**4.5 Rules.** §2.6: external data and models are acceptable unless the host prohibits them, if free and equally
accessible. OAI itself stays closed for us (`94e7b54`). Public notebooks now cite OAI-trained weights, fastMRI+ and
SKM-TEA; whether the host has ruled since 09-26 is not re-checked.

## 6. Outlook, in numbers

Five folds are at 0.945: a deficit of 12 × 0.055 = 0.66 AUROC-points. 0.97 is a deficit of 0.36.
- If the vision work lifts Lateral OA and PF OA to what the report already reads on gold (0.87, 0.905) and MCL / ACL
  likewise: about +0.10 to +0.14 points = **+0.008 to +0.012 macro** → ~0.953–0.957.
- The remaining gap to 0.97 is ~0.17 points, and it sits in Lateral Meniscus and Synovitis plus whatever the OA
  compartments lack beyond the report. The text does not contain it (the best report reader is 0.87 and 0.79 there).
  Only image-read labels do: OAI's MOAKS grades, which is what the 0.949 public model and the ≈0.957 notebooks carry.
- #1 on the board is 0.966.

## 7. Not proposed, and why

- Bigger backbones: ConvNeXt-small beat ViT-L by 0.011 at a fifth of the cost.
- Centre crops and zoom: three attempts, and the public crop-trained model is below its own full view (§4.1).
- More same-label teacher rounds: closed 10-04 (a student of a same-report teacher relearns the report).
- A frontier-LLM re-read of the reports: the report ceiling on gold is ~0.90 and we are at 0.93.
- Scanner or site inputs: §2.4, and the site prior already lost (−0.0057).
- fastMRI+ as an auxiliary task in the ten days left: 314 GB removed from this Mac on 10-09, coronal only, and a
  dense-box head to write. It is the open-data route to image labels if the timeline were longer.
